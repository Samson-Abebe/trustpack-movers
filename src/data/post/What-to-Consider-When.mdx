## 1. Proven Asset Responsibility & Safety Standards

The most critical baseline for any moving or cleaning company is how carefully they handle your property. Unprofessional movers or untrained janitorial staff frequently cause accidental breakages, scratches, and lost inventory.

### Questions to Ask:
*   *Does the company take full accountability for cargo in transit?*
*   *Do they manufacture and supply their own specialized packaging materials?*

We supply specialized packaging materials—including multi-layer corrugated boxes, custom bubble wrap, and protective plaster—to ensure that everything from fragile glassware to large 65”–100” smart displays and IT servers arrives without a scratch.

## 2. Service Integration & Vendor Consolidation

Managing separate vendors for packing, heavy freight, site loading, and post-move cleanup multiplies your administrative burden and creates coordination bottlenecks.

### Benefits of an Integrated Partner:
*   **Single Point of Contact:** Coordinate relocation transport, furniture reassembly, and deep sanitation through one dedicated service manager.
*   **Cost Efficiency:** Streamline billing and unlock bundled package rates by combining transportation with facility maintenance.

## 4. Established Route Knowledge & Fleet Availability

A logistics company is only as reliable as its fleet and corridor experience. If a provider lacks dedicated vehicles or route familiarity, schedules stall during peak highway traffic.

Ensure your provider operates dedicated vehicles—such as KiYa and Eicher commercial trucks—and has proven experience along major commercial arteries (including the **Addis Ababa–Adama corridor** and regional city routes). Fleet scalability (such as expanding fleet targets to match high-volume demand) ensures your cargo is never left waiting for an available vehicle.

## 5. Pricing Transparency & Quotation Policies

Avoid service providers that offer vague initial estimates only to tack on hidden charges for fuel, floor steps, loading labor, or packaging materials after the job is completed.

A trustworthy partner operates on a **transparent, quotation-based system** calculated using concrete operational variables:

*   **Logistics Pricing Variables:** Distance/route, cargo type/volume, vehicle class, floor heights, and packaging requirements.
*   **Cleaning Pricing Variables:** Square footage of premises, property condition, cleaning frequency (daily/weekly/monthly), and required manpower/consumables.
