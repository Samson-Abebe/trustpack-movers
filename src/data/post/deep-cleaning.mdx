## 1. What Is Regular Cleaning?

Regular cleaning consists of routine surface maintenance designed to keep your home or workplace presentable, hygienic, and organized on an ongoing basis. It prevents daily dust buildup and handles standard housekeeping tasks.

### Key Tasks Included in Regular Cleaning:
*   **Surface Dusting & Wiping:** Desks, tables, counter surfaces, and accessible furniture.
*   **Floor Care:** Sweeping, vacuuming, and standard mopping of hard floors.
*   **Routine Sanitation:** Wiping down washrooms, sinks, mirrors, and replenishing supplies.
*   **Waste Management:** Emptying trash bins and replacing liners across office workstations or home rooms.

Regular cleaning is typically scheduled on a **daily, weekly, or bi-weekly basis** under flexible contract-based service arrangements.



## 2. What Is Deep Cleaning?

Deep cleaning goes far beyond surface-level care. It targets hidden dirt, stubborn grime, buildup behind appliances, high-touch germ zones, and hard-to-reach structural surfaces that standard cleaning omits.

> **The TrustPack Cleaning Standard:** Deep cleaning is an intensive, detailed process requiring extra manpower, specialized equipment, and comprehensive techniques to restore a property to pristine condition.

### Key Tasks Included in Deep Cleaning:
*   **Detailed Wall & Surface Scrubbing:** Removing scuffs, grease, and built-up grime from walls, baseboards, doors, and window frames.
*   **Comprehensive Washroom Sanitation:** Deep scrubbing of tile grout, acid-washing fixtures, and total disinfection of sanitary facilities.
*   **Kitchen & Appliance Deep Cleans:** Scrubbing grease traps, cleaning behind heavy equipment/appliances, and sanitizing cabinets inside and out.
*   **Pre- and Post-Move Cleaning:** Complete environmental reset before moving into a new villa, apartment, or commercial office space.

Deep cleaning is recommended on a **quarterly, bi-annual, or annual basis**, or as a specialized setup prior to moving.



## 3. Side-by-Side Comparison

Understanding the technical scope of each service helps you optimize maintenance spending and avoid unnecessary costs:

| Service Feature | Regular Maintenance Cleaning | Intensive Deep Cleaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Goal** | Preserve daily hygiene & appearance | Eliminate deep-set grime & restore spaces |
| **Focus Areas** | High-visibility, accessible surfaces | Hidden corners, grout, high fixtures, & appliances |
| **Frequency** | Daily, weekly, or monthly | Quarterly, seasonally, or pre/post relocation |
| **Execution Time** | Fast (1–3 hours depending on property size) | Thorough, multi-hour intensive process |
| **Cost Efficiency** | Lower per-visit cost via recurring contracts | Higher single-visit investment for long-term protection |



## 4. When Does Your Property Need a Deep Clean?

Not sure which service level to select? You should schedule a professional deep clean if:

1.  **You Are Moving Locations:** Transitioning between offices or homes requires an intensive pre/post-move clean. Combine this with our **Corporate Relocation Package** to move and clean in one seamless operation.
2.  **Post-Renovation or Construction:** Dust from masonry, painting, or woodwork settles into every crevice and requires professional extraction.
3.  **Seasonal Refresh:** High-traffic commercial buildings benefit from periodic deep sanitization to protect employee health and reduce sick leave.
4.  **Before Establishing a Maintenance Plan:** Starting routine regular cleaning on a deeply soiled space is inefficient. A initial deep clean resets the space, making ongoing regular cleaning far more cost-effective.
