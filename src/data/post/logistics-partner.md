## 5 Essential Factors when Evaluating a Logistics Partner

### 1. Proven Responsibility & Property Safety
Logistics failures often manifest as broken equipment, lost inventory, or damaged assets. A reliable partner must back their claims with concrete handling standards and proper protective equipment.

> **The TrustPack Standard:** We provide specialized packaging materials—including heavy-duty cardboard, custom bubble wrap, and protective plaster—to safeguard delicate electronics (such as 65”–100” smart displays), office furniture, and fragile goods.

### 2. Service Integration & Vendor Consolidation
Managing separate vendors for packing, heavy transit, loading, and post-move cleanup multiplies administrative overhead. 

Opt for an integrated provider that manages both operational logistics and facility maintenance. Through our two core divisions—**TrustPack Logistics & Transportation** and **TrustPack Professional Cleaning Services**—businesses gain a single point of contact for complete relocation and workspace preparation.

### 3. Operational Speed & Downtime Reduction
Prolonged relocations interrupt operations and hurt revenue. Evaluate potential partners based on their ability to execute rapid, organized turnarounds.

*   **Execution Capacity:** What takes days for internal teams to pack and coordinate is executed by TrustPack’s professional teams in just **4 hours**.
*   **Complete Lifecycle:** Look for end-to-end service, including disassembly, secure loading, highway transport, unloading, and final reassembly.

### 4. Route Experience & Fleet Availability
Ensure your provider has established route knowledge along your primary supply chain corridors. TrustPack operates dedicated logistics pipelines across the **Addis Ababa–Adama corridor** and regional destinations, supported by a growing transportation fleet designed for commercial freight.

### 5. Price Transparency
Avoid providers that offer vague estimates only to add hidden fees for labor, mileage, or loading. Look for a partner offering itemized, quotation-based pricing based on distance, cargo volume, vehicle type, and packaging requirements.
