## The True Cost of Unreliable Logistics

Many growing enterprises underestimate how much fragmented, unreliable transport impacts their bottom line. Coordinating multiple third-party movers, dealing with local handling delays, and suffering property damage creates hidden operational drains:

*   **Excess Downtime:** Days spent packing, shipping, and setting up an office derail team productivity and halt business operations.
*   **Property & Asset Damage:** Improper handling of high-value equipment—such as 65”–100” smart displays, servers, and executive furniture—leads to unexpected repair or replacement costs.
*   **Customer Dissatisfaction:** Unreliable supply chain transport along key trading routes jeopardizes client trust and contract renewals.
